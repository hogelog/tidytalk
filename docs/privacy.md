# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-31_

TidyTalk (`org.hogel.tidytalk`) is an Android app that helps you find and delete
files and unused apps to free up storage.

**TidyTalk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** The app has
no internet permission, no analytics, no ads, and no crash reporting. Nothing
you see or do in the app leaves your device through the app.

## What the app accesses on your device

All access below happens locally on your device. Results are shown only inside
the app and are never sent anywhere by TidyTalk.

- **Files and folders** (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`): to compute storage usage,
  list and preview files, and delete the files you choose to remove.
- **Installed apps** (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, `PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`): to list your
  installed apps and their last-used dates so you can spot apps you no longer
  use.
- **Uninstall apps** (`REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES`): to let you uninstall an app you
  select. The uninstall itself is handled by Android's standard system dialog.

## Settings storage

Your in-app settings are stored locally on your device. TidyTalk has no user
accounts and no server.

## Use of an external AI service (bring your own AI)

TidyTalk does not include any AI service. Instead, it generates a text prompt
that lists file paths, sizes, and dates — **not** the contents of your files.
You may choose to copy this prompt into a third-party AI chat service (such as
ChatGPT or Claude) and paste its answer back into TidyTalk.

This copy-and-paste is an action you take manually, outside of TidyTalk. Any
data you send to such a service is governed by **that service's** privacy
policy, not this one. TidyTalk itself never contacts any AI service.

## Children

TidyTalk is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same
